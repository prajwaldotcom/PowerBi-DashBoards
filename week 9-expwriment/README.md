📊 Exploratory Data Analysis on Business Overview
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) dashboard for business performance using Power BI.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales, profit, orders, quantity, profit margin, categories, regions, segments, sub-categories, and shipping modes. It helps understand which areas of the business are performing well and where improvement may be needed.

The dashboard is designed to make business data easier to understand through interactive KPIs, charts, graphs, and filters.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall business performance.
Understand total sales and profit.
Identify the best-performing product categories.
Compare sales across different regions.
Analyze sales by customer segment.
Understand sales performance by sub-category.
Analyze sales based on shipping mode.
Track sales trends across different years.
Identify areas with high and low profitability.
Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

🧮 Important Measures

Examples of business measures used in the dashboard include:

Total Sales = SUM(Sales)

Total Profit = SUM(Profit)

Total Quantity = SUM(Quantity)

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Order ID)

Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales

These measures help calculate the main business performance indicators displayed on the dashboard.
