Finance Budget Analysis KPI Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

The Finance Budget Analysis KPI Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and compare budgeted amounts, actual spending, transactions, departmental performance, monthly trends, yearly performance, payment methods, and spending categories.

The dashboard provides finance teams and management with a clear view of how actual financial activity compares with planned budgets and helps identify important spending patterns and variances.

🎯 Objectives

Monitor total budget and actual spending.

Compare budgeted amounts with actual expenditure.

Analyze financial performance by department.

Track budget and actual amounts month by month.

Compare yearly budget and actual spending.

Analyze transaction volume.

Understand transaction distribution by payment method.

Identify categories with the highest budget allocation.

Provide an interactive dashboard for quick financial decision-making.

📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard contains the following KPI cards:

KPI

Description

Total Budget Amount

Total amount planned/budgeted across the dataset.

Total Actual Amount

Total amount actually spent/recorded.

Count of Transaction

Total number of financial transactions.

Variance of Budget

Difference/variance associated with budget values.

Variance of Actual

Difference/variance associated with actual spending values.

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

1. Actual Spend and Total Budget by Department

Chart Type: Clustered Column Chart

Purpose:
Compares actual spending against the total budget for each department.

Fields used:

X-Axis: Department

Y-Axis / Values: Actual Spend, Total Budget

Legend: Actual Spend and Total Budget

Business Insight:
This visualization helps identify departments where actual spending is higher or lower than the allocated budget.

2. Total Actual and Budget Amount by Month

Chart Type: Line Chart

Purpose:
Shows monthly changes in actual spending and budget amounts.

Fields used:

X-Axis: Month

Y-Axis / Values: Total Actual Amount, Budget Amount

Legend: Total Actual and Budget Amount

Business Insight:
Helps identify monthly spending trends, increases, decreases, and periods where actual spending differs significantly from the budget.

3. Department Filter

Chart Type: Slicer

Purpose:
Allows users to filter the dashboard according to a selected department.

Departments available:

Finance

HR

IT

Marketing

Operations

Sales

Business Insight:
Users can analyze the complete dashboard for an individual department instead of viewing all departments together.

4. Budget and Actual Spend by Year

Chart Type: Line and Clustered Column Chart

Purpose:
Compares yearly budget amounts with actual spending.

Fields used:

X-Axis: Year

Column Values: Sum of Budget Amount

Line Values: Sum of Actual Amount

Years displayed:

2021

2022

2023

Business Insight:
Provides a yearly overview of financial performance and helps determine whether actual spending is increasing or decreasing compared with planned budgets.

5. Count of Transaction by Payment Method

Chart Type: Donut Chart

Purpose:
Shows the proportion of transactions made through different payment methods.

Fields used:

Legend: Payment Method

Values: Count of Transaction

Payment methods displayed:

UPI

Card

Cash

Bank Transfer

Business Insight:
Helps understand which payment methods are most frequently used.

6. Sum of Budget Amount by Category

Chart Type: Horizontal Bar Chart

Purpose:
Ranks categories according to their allocated budget amount.

Fields used:

Y-Axis: Category

X-Axis / Values: Sum of Budget Amount

Categories displayed include:

Travel

Utilities

Training

Marketing

Infrastructure

Salaries

Business Insight:
Helps management identify which business categories receive the largest budget allocations.

🧩 Dashboard Interactivity

The dashboard supports interactive filtering through the Department slicer.

When a department is selected:

KPI values are updated.

Department-level charts are filtered.

Monthly trends are updated.

Yearly budget and actual values are updated.

Transaction-related visuals are updated according to the selected context.

This allows users to perform both overall financial analysis and department-specific analysis.

📐 Analysis Performed

The dashboard performs the following major analysis:

Budget vs Actual Analysis

Compares planned financial allocations with actual spending.

Department Analysis

Evaluates financial activity across Finance, HR, IT, Marketing, Operations, and Sales.

Monthly Trend Analysis

Identifies changes in budget and actual amounts throughout the year.

Yearly Analysis

Compares financial performance across different years.

Transaction Analysis

Measures the total number of transactions and their distribution by payment method.

Category Analysis

Identifies categories receiving the highest budget allocations.
